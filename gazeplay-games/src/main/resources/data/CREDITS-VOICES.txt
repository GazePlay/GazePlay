Voices of the games of GazePlay Learning (gazeplay-games: data), November 2026.

The questions said by the games "Where is...?" (whereIsTheAnimal, whereIsTheAnimalDynamic, whereIsTheColor,
whereIsTheFlag, whereIsTheLetter, whereIsTheNumber, whereIsTheShape), "Find the odd one out" (findTheOddOneOut) and
"Identify the letters" (literacy) are made by speech synthesis, offline, with the text the game shows: 944 files, in
French and English, a woman's voice (".w." or "f") and a man's voice (".m." or "m"), chosen in the settings.
They were made on 20/11/2026 by gradle/images/make_voices.py; gradle/images/voix.csv gives the voice and the text of
each file. They replace voices of unknown source, or made by services whose terms were not checked (Amazon Polly,
ttsmp3.com). See INVENTAIRE_SONS.md.

Synthesizer: Piper 1.8.0 (https://github.com/OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl), GPL-3.0-or-later. Only the sounds it made are
shipped, not the synthesizer nor its voice models.
