#
# "circuit <level> <columns> <lines> <tunnel x,y>" starts a circuit: its level (easy, normal, hard), the size of its grid,
# and the box of the tunnel the trains come out of.
# Then one line per stretch of rails: "<from> <x,y> ... <to>".
# - <from>: T (the tunnel) or the letter of a switch; the stretch starts at its box.
# - <x,y>: the boxes where the rails go, in straight lines (each one in the same line or the same column as the last).
# - <to>: the letter of a switch, which stands on the last box, or a number: a station on the last box.
# A switch has two stretches going out of it. The tests check that the rails never cross, and that each station can be
# reached.
