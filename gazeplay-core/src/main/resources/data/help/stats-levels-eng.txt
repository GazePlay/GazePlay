# What this chart is for
In the variants whose difficulty adapts to the player (for instance the Dynamic variants), it shows the level reached at each round.

# How to read it
- At the bottom: the number of the round.
- On the left: the level. The higher it is, the harder the round.
- The solid line: the level of each round.
- The dashed line: the average level.
- The dotted lines: the average plus and minus the standard deviation.
- A click on the chart enlarges it, another click puts it back.
