# What this image is for
It shows where the gaze, or the mouse, went during the game, over a picture of the screen taken at the start of the game.

# The heat map
- The colored spots are the places looked at. The longer a place was looked at, the warmer its color.
- The legend on the right gives the scale: the number of positions recorded at that place. The colors and the opacity can be changed in the settings.

# The path of the gaze
- The numbered circles are the fixations, the places where the gaze or the mouse stopped. The number gives the order: 1 for the first one.
- The bigger a circle, the longer the fixation lasted compared with the others.
- The lines join the fixations in their order.
- The circles are blue for the gaze and red for the mouse.
- This path can be turned off in the settings.

# The buttons
- Mouse, Gaze, Mouse and Gaze: choose what is shown, according to the input mode of the game.
- A click on the image enlarges it, another click puts it back.

# During an evaluation
This image gathers the whole session. The heatmaps folder of the session also holds a map for each screen, over the image of that screen.
