# What this chart is for
It shows where the fixations were during the game: the places where the gaze, or the mouse, stopped.

# How to read it
- At the bottom: the time, in seconds since the first fixation.
- On the left: the position, in pixels of the screen.
- The solid line: the horizontal position (from left to right).
- The dashed line: the vertical position (from top to bottom).
- A step shows a long fixation at the same place; a jump shows the gaze moving somewhere else.
- A click on the chart enlarges it, another click puts it back.

# When it shows
Only when the path of the gaze is recorded (settings, Statistics section) and the game has fixations.
