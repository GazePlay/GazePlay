# Preparing an evaluation
Evaluations are created on the GazePlay Eval website, opened by the "Create an evaluation (GazePlay Eval website)" card. Download the .zip file of the evaluation, then import it with "Import an evaluation".
Before a session, "Test an evaluation" checks the calibration of the eye tracker: the evaluation is played without the form, and nothing is recorded.

# Running an evaluation
- "Launch an evaluation", then choose the evaluation.
- Fill in the participant form, then press "Start".
- The participant chooses a picture by looking at it: a ring fills around it during the fixation.
- To leave before the end: the gear at the bottom left, then the home button. The results obtained so far are saved, and the session is marked as not finished. While the gear menu is open, the evaluation is paused.

# Examiner keys
During a screen (instruction, grid, transition):
- Space: go to the next screen.
- P: play the video or the sound of the instruction again, or the sound of the grid (if there is one).
On the "Restart / Continue" screen (after a grid, in every mode except Classic):
- R or left arrow: restart the grid.
- C or right arrow: continue.
- Enter: confirm the choice proposed by the participant (otherwise: continue).
To leave: the gear at the bottom left.

# Ways of taking an evaluation (settings, Evaluations section)
- Classic: the screens follow one another without stopping.
- Autonomy with oral support: after each grid, the examiner chooses to restart or to continue (with the mouse or the keyboard). The gaze of the participant on the two buttons is recorded.
- Autonomy with visual support: the participant points at a button with the gaze, then the examiner confirms with Enter.
- Total autonomy: the participant chooses alone, with the gaze.

# Other useful settings
- Delay before a selection is possible: at the start of each screen, nothing can be chosen during this delay (1 second by default).
- The sound of a selected cell blocks the other selections while it plays.
- The fixation length (Eye tracker section) is how long a picture must be looked at to be chosen.

# What is recorded
Each session has its folder in Documents\GazePlayLearning_Eval ("Results of the evaluations" button):
- session.json: the participant, the evaluation and the settings of the session;
- resultats.csv or .xlsx: the answers, screen by screen;
- evenements.csv: each entry and exit of the gaze or the mouse on a picture, each selection, each key;
- echantillons.csv: all the positions of the gaze and of the mouse;
- heatmaps: the heat map of each screen.
The information about the participant is written in clear text: protect this folder.
