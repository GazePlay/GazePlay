# What this chart is for
It shows the time it took to reach each goal of the game, for instance a bubble burst, a target hit or an image selected. It shows whether the player goes faster or slower during the game.

# How to read it
- At the bottom: the number of the goal, in the order of the game. In the shooting games, the times are sorted, from the shortest to the longest.
- On the left: the time, in milliseconds (1,000 milliseconds make 1 second).
- The red curve: the time for each goal. The lower it is, the faster the goal was reached.
- The green line: the average time.
- The grey lines: the standard deviation, around the average. The further they are from the green line, the more irregular the times.
- A click on the chart enlarges it, another click puts it back.

# The other views
Depending on the game, buttons at the bottom of the screen replace this chart:
- Color Bands: the position of the fixations during the game, in width (X) and in height (Y).
- Levels Info: the level reached at each round, in the games whose level adapts to the player, with its average and standard deviation.
- Chi2 Info: a table of a statistical test (chi-squared) on the levels, for analyses.
