# What this chart is for
It shows the time it took to reach each goal of the game, for instance a bubble burst, a target hit or an image selected. It shows whether the player goes faster or slower during the game.

# How to read it
- At the bottom: the number of the goal, in the order of the game. In the shooting games, the times are sorted, from the shortest to the longest.
- On the left: the time, in seconds.
- The solid line, in the main color: the time for each goal. The lower it is, the faster the goal was reached.
- The dashed line: the average time.
- The dotted lines: the average plus and minus the standard deviation. The further they are from the average, the more irregular the times.
- A click on the chart enlarges it, another click puts it back.

# The other views
In the advanced mode, tabs above the chart show the other views of the game, only those with data: the heat map, the positions of the gaze, the levels and the chi-squared test (variants whose level adapts to the player).
