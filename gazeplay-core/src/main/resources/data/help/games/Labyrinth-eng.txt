# The principle
Guide the mouse to the cheese (or the animal to its house) in a labyrinth of hedges!

# How to play
- The labyrinth is dug before your eyes, then the mouse appears in a corner and the cheese further away. There is only one path between two boxes.
- Arrows around the character: fixate an arrow next to the mouse, it goes one box that way. A gray arrow shows a hedge: it does nothing.
- Arrows around the labyrinth: fixate one of the four big arrows around the labyrinth. Keep looking at it to go on.
- Choose then guide: fixate the mouse to choose it (it shines), then look at the next box where it must go: it goes there at once. Looking further away lets it go.
- A ring fills during a fixation; its length is the one of the settings.
- At the top, the cheeses (or the houses) already reached.
- Nothing can be chosen while the labyrinth is dug (1.5 seconds), nor before the delay of the settings. Nothing moves while the menu (Esc key) is open.

# How to win
Each cheese reached counts a point, then a new labyrinth is dug. After 3, 5 or 10 cheeses, it is "Bravo", then the statistics. Without end, the game goes on until you leave it by the menu (Esc key).

# The variants
Choose a variant (it is circled), then press "Start".
- 3 times, 5 times, 10 times or without end: the number of cheeses (or houses) to reach.
- At the bottom, the character: the mouse and the cheese, or an animal at random and the house.
- At the bottom too, the level: Easy, a labyrinth of 3 lines and 5 columns; Normal, 5 lines and 7 columns; Hard, 7 lines and 9 columns.
- Last, the way of moving: arrows around the character, arrows around the labyrinth, or choose then guide.
