# The principle
Take care of the Biboule: feed it, wash it and play with it.

# How to play
- At the top, three gauges: Hunger, Cleanliness and Play. They empty slowly (a full gauge empties in 2 minutes).
- When a gauge is almost empty, it blinks, the Biboule is sad and a bubble shows what it wants.
- The buttons on the left open a room (look at a button until the ring is full):
- Living room: the hand follows your gaze; stroke the Biboule, it is happy.
- Kitchen: look at the bowl to fill the spoon, then bring the spoon to the mouth of the Biboule.
- Bathroom: the shower follows your gaze; keep it above the Biboule to wash it.
- Garden: the Biboule flies; touch it with the ball to play with it.
- Each activity done fills its gauge by a quarter.

# How to win
There is nothing to win: keep the Biboule happy, as long as you like. The game does not stop by itself: leave with the gear at the bottom left.

# The variants
This game has no variant: press "Start the game".
