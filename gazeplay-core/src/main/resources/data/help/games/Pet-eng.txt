# The principle
Take care of the Biboule: feed it, wash it and play with it.

# How to play
- At the top, three gauges: Hunger, Cleanliness and Play. They empty slowly (a full gauge empties in 2 minutes).
- When a gauge is almost empty, it blinks, the Biboule is sad and a bubble shows what it wants.
- The buttons on the left open a room (look at a button until the ring is full):
- Living room: the hand follows your gaze; stroke the Biboule, it is happy and chirps.
- Kitchen: on the table, four dishes: the bowl of mash, an apple, a banana and a cake. Look at a dish to take it (the spoon fills with mash, or you take the fruit or the cake), then bring it to the mouth of the Biboule: it crunches.
- Bathroom: the shower follows your gaze; keep it above the Biboule to wash it (the water is heard).
- Garden: at the bottom right, three toys: the ball, the juggling balls and the hoop. Look at a toy until the ring is full to take it. The Biboule flies; touch it with the toy to play: the ball bounces, the balls turn above its head, the hoop comes down around it, spinning.
- Each activity done fills its gauge by a quarter, with three little musical notes.

# How to win
There is nothing to win: keep the Biboule happy, as long as you like. The game does not stop by itself: leave by the menu (Esc key).

# The variants
This game has no variant: press "Start the game".
