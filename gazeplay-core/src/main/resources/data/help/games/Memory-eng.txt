# The principle
Find the pairs of identical cards.

# How to play
- Look at a card: a ring goes around the card, then it turns over (fixation length of the settings).
- Look at a second card. If they are the same, they are circled in green, then they disappear.
- Otherwise they are circled in red, stay shown for a moment, then turn back: remember where they are.
- At the top, "Pairs" shows how many pairs you found.

# How to win
When all the pairs are found: "Bravo", then a new game starts. The game does not stop by itself: leave with the gear at the bottom left.

# The variants
Choose a variant (it is framed), then press "Start".
- The buttons, the size: 4, 6 or 12 cards, or Dynamic: the number of cards changes every 3 games after your tries (more cards if you find them fast, fewer if it is hard).
- At the bottom, what the cards show: Pictures, Letters, Digits or My pictures (your own pictures, to put in the settings, Folders section, "Images of the games").
- At the very bottom: Hidden cards (you have to remember) or Visible cards (all the cards are face up: just find the pairs).
