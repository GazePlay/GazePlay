# The principle
Play rock, paper, scissors against the computer! The rock breaks the scissors, the paper wraps the rock, the scissors cut the paper.

# How to play
- At the bottom, three cards: the rock, the paper and the scissors. Fixate a card to play its sign.
- Against the computer: after your choice, "Rock... paper... scissors!" while both fists swing, then both signs show. The winning hand grows, circled in green with a check; the losing hand is crossed out in red (yours shakes); a draw shows a yellow "=". The text says "You win!", "You lose" or "Draw". The result stays: look at the arrow on the right to go to the next round (or the adult beside you clicks it).
- To learn the rule: the sign of the computer is shown; choose the one that beats it. A mistake crosses your hand out in red, makes it shake, tells why (for example "The paper wraps the rock") and greys your card: try again. A right answer circles your hand in green with a check; look at the arrow on the right for the next question.
- A ring fills during a fixation; it lasts the length of the settings.
- At the top, the score (or the right answers).
- Nothing can be chosen before the delay of the settings, nor while the round is shown; at the start of each new round, nothing can be chosen during this delay (Games, "Delay before a selection is possible when new choices appear"). Nothing moves while the menu (Escape key) is open.

# How to win
Against the computer, the first one to win 2, 3 or 5 rounds wins the game: if it is you, it is "Bravo", then the statistics; if it is the computer, the statistics at once. To learn the rule, after 5 or 10 right answers, it is "Bravo". Without end, the game goes on until you leave it with the menu (Escape key).

# The variants
Choose a variant (it is circled), then press "Start".
- At the bottom, the mode: against the computer (its sign hidden until the end), or learn the rule (its sign shown).
- Against the computer: 2, 3 or 5 rounds to win, or without end.
- To learn the rule: 5 or 10 right answers, or without end.
