# The principle
Play rock, paper, scissors against the computer! The rock breaks the scissors, the paper wraps the rock, the scissors cut the paper.

# How to play
- At the bottom, three cards: the rock, the paper and the scissors. Fixate a card to play its sign.
- Against the computer: after your choice, "Rock... paper... scissors!" while both fists swing, then both signs show. The winning hand grows: "You win!", "You lose" or "Draw".
- To learn the rule: the sign of the computer is shown; choose the one that beats it. A mistake tells why (for example "The paper wraps the rock") and greys your card: try again.
- A ring fills during a fixation; it lasts the length of the settings.
- At the top, the score (or the right answers).
- Nothing can be chosen before the delay of the settings, nor while the round is shown. Nothing moves while the menu (Escape key) is open.

# How to win
Against the computer, the first one to win 2, 3 or 5 rounds wins the game: if it is you, it is "Bravo", then the statistics; if it is the computer, the statistics at once. To learn the rule, after 5 or 10 right answers, it is "Bravo". Without end, the game goes on until you leave it with the menu (Escape key).

# The variants
Choose a variant (it is circled), then press "Start".
- At the bottom, the mode: against the computer (its sign hidden until the end), or learn the rule (its sign shown).
- Against the computer: 2, 3 or 5 rounds to win, or without end.
- To learn the rule: 5 or 10 right answers, or without end.
