# The principle
Color a black and white drawing, area by area, with your gaze.

# How to play
- Choose a color in the palette on the right by looking at it: it is circled with the main color.
- Look at an area of the drawing: a ring fills, then the area takes the chosen color. With the mouse, a click is enough.
- The palette button pauses the coloring (to look at the drawing without coloring it) or resumes it.
- "Other colors" opens a window of 15 more colors.
- "Undo" undoes the last coloring (up to 10 times); "Start the drawing again" makes it white again.
- The arrows above the drawing change the drawing ("3 / 8" shows where you are).
- At the bottom of the palette, the floppy disk keeps the drawing as a picture in the drawings folder of GazePlay (Documents, GazePlay_Learning, dessins), without asking anything; it opens from the settings, Folders, "My drawings (Scribble and Coloring)". The printer prints it. Both buttons can be hidden in the settings (Games).
- At the start, nothing can be colored or chosen during the delay of the settings (Games, "Delay before a selection is possible when new choices appear").

# How to win
There is no end: color as you like, then go to the next drawing. Each area colored counts one point. Leave by the menu (Esc key).

# The variants
Choose a variant (it is framed), then press "Start".
- Easy: simple drawings, with 1 or 2 areas to color (8 drawings).
- Medium: 3 to 6 areas (8 drawings).
- Hard: 7 areas or more (8 drawings).
- My drawings: your own drawings, to put in the settings (Folders section, "Drawings to color").
