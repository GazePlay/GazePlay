# The principle
Listen to the melody of the device, then repeat it by looking at its buttons! Each button has its color, its symbol (triangle, star, square, circle) and its note.

# How to play
- While the device plays ("Listen" in the middle), its buttons light up one after the other with their notes. Nothing can be chosen meanwhile.
- When it is your turn ("Your turn"), fixate the buttons in the same order. A button chosen lights up and plays its note.
- A mistake shows at once: a low sound, and the right button blinks. Then the device plays the melody again.
- After each melody repeated, the device plays it again with one more note.
- A ring fills during a fixation; it lasts the length of the settings.
- In the middle, the number of notes of the melody. At the top left, the lives (a red heart for each life); at the top, the notes.
- The notes go at the speed of the settings.
- Nothing can be chosen before the delay of the settings. Nothing moves while the menu (Escape key) is open.

# How to win
When the melody has 5, 8 or 12 notes and you repeated it, it is "Melody complete!", then "Bravo" and the statistics. In the classic mode, you have 3 lives: each mistake costs one; at the third one, the game stops and shows your longest melody, then the statistics. Without end, the melody grows until you have no life left.

# The variants
Choose a variant (it is circled), then press "Start".
- 5 notes, 8 notes, 12 notes or without end: the length of the melody to reach.
- At the bottom, the mode: classic (the device chooses the notes, 3 lives); two players (each one in turn repeats the whole melody, then adds a note of their choice; a mistake and the other player wins); free (you choose a note, the device plays the whole melody again; no mistake possible, to discover the game).
