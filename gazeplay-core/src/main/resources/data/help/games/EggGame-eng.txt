# The principle
An egg waits in its nest. Look at it: it cracks, then it hatches. Who will come out?

# How to play
- Look at the egg: a ring goes round it (fixation length of the settings), then it cracks ("crack") and shakes.
- Stay on the egg: it cracks again, without having to look away.
- At the last step, the top of the shell flies off and an animal comes out of the egg (chick, duckling, turtle, crocodile, dinosaur...), or one of your images.
- At the top, "Hatched" counts the eggs hatched.

# How to win
Each egg hatched is a success: a new egg comes next. The game does not stop by itself: leave it by the gear at the bottom left.

# The variants
Choose a variant (it is framed), then press "Start".
- 2, 3, 4 or 5 steps: the number of fixations hatching an egg.
- At the bottom, what comes out of the eggs:
- Animals: animals born from an egg.
- My images: your images. Add them in the settings (Folders, "Images of the games") or from the window of the variants.
