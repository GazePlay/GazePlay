# The principle
A digit is asked for (from 0 to 9): find it among the others.

# How to play
- The question is written in the middle of the screen and said aloud, then it goes down to the band at the bottom.
- The pictures appear. Look at the right answer: a ring goes around the picture while you look at it, and the picture is chosen when it is full (fixation length of the settings).
- When the pictures appear, nothing can be chosen during the delay of the settings (Games, "Delay before a selection is possible when new choices appear").
- To hear the question again, look at the loudspeaker of the band.

# How to win
- Right answer: the picture is circled in green, it grows, then "Bravo" and a new question.
- Wrong answer: the picture is crossed out and greyed, and cannot be chosen again; look again (the question is said again if the settings ask for it). If the question has a sound, a bubble reminds you that you can hear it again with the loudspeaker.
- With or without end, with "With hearts", you have 3 hearts at the top left: each wrong answer costs one. With no heart left, "Lost!", then the statistics. With "Without hearts", there are no hearts and the game is never lost: a wrong answer only counts a mistake in the statistics.
- Depending on the variant, the game stops after 5, 10 or 15 screens found: "Bravo", then the statistics; without end, leave by the menu (Esc key).
- At the bottom left, the counter shows the number of the current screen: "Screen 3 / 10", or "Screen 7" without end.

# The variants
Choose a variant (it is framed), then press "Start".
- The size of the grid: 2, 4, 6 or 9 pictures. The more pictures, the harder.
- At the bottom, the end of the game: 5, 10 or 15 screens, or without end.
- At the bottom, the example: Learning (the picture of the right answer is shown at the right of the question, and stays beside it while you choose), Training (the picture is shown at the right of the question, then goes away once the pictures are shown) or Mastery (the question alone, without example).
- At the bottom, the hearts: With hearts (3 hearts) or Without hearts.
