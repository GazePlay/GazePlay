# The principle
A race of frogs on the pond: catch bugs to make your frog move forward.

# How to play
- Bugs fly in the sky. Look at a bug: the frog of the bank shoots its tongue and catches it (no need to look long).
- For each bug caught, your frog jumps one step. It is the green frog, on the top lane marked "You".
- The three other frogs jump by themselves.

# How to win
- It takes 20 bugs to reach the checkered finish line.
- The first frog to arrive wins. The ranking is shown, with "Bravo" if you won, then a new race starts.
- The races follow one another: leave by the menu (Esc key).

# The variants
Choose a variant (it is framed), then press "Start".
- Easy: the other frogs take between 1 min 30 and 1 min 50 to arrive; you have time (20 bugs to catch).
- Normal: they take between 1 min and 1 min 15 to arrive; do not dawdle.
- Hard: they take between 40 and 50 seconds to arrive; catch the bugs without waiting.
