# The principle
A country is asked for: find its flag among the others.

# How to play
- The question is written in the middle of the screen and said aloud, then it goes down to the band at the bottom.
- The pictures appear. Look at the right answer: a ring goes around the picture while you look at it, and the picture is chosen when it is full (fixation length of the settings).
- To hear the question again, look at the loudspeaker of the band.

# How to win
- Right answer: the picture is circled in green, it grows, then "Bravo" and a new question.
- Wrong answer: the picture is crossed out and greyed; look again (the question is said again if the settings ask for it).
- The game does not stop by itself: leave with the gear at the bottom left, or set a time or score limit in the settings (Games section).

# The variants
- At the top, the countries: Most famous, Africa, America, Asia, Europe or All flags.
- The size of the grid: 2, 4, 6 or 9 pictures. The more pictures, the harder.
