# The principle
A picture is hidden under panes of glass. Bubbles go by: each bubble burst takes a pane away. Find the whole picture!

# How to play
- Look at a bubble to burst it (with a "pop").
- Depending on the variant, it bursts as soon as you look at it, or after a fixation: a ring goes round it (fixation length of the settings).
- At the top, "12 / 36" counts the panes taken away.
- The speed of the bubbles follows the animation speed of the settings.
- Nothing moves while the menu of the gear is open.

# How to win
When the 36 panes are taken away: "Bravo", then a new hidden picture. The game does not stop by itself: leave it by the gear at the bottom left.

# The variants
Choose a variant (it is framed), then press "Start".
- The bubbles go up, down, to the right, to the left, or stay still (they move to another place from time to time).
- At the bottom, how they burst: as soon as looked at, or after a fixation.
- At the bottom too, what they show:
- Colors: bubbles of all colors; the hidden pictures are underwater scenes.
- Sea animals: an animal in each bubble; the same hidden pictures.
- My images: your images in the bubbles, and they are the hidden pictures. Add them in the settings (Folders, "Images of the games") or from the window of the variants.
