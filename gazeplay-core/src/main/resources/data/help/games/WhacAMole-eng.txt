# The principle
Moles come out of their holes. Touch them with your eyes before they go back!

# How to play
- As chosen at the bottom of the window, the target is touched as soon as you look at it, or after a fixation: a ring fills on it. Its length is that of the settings, also changed in the menu (Esc key).
- If you leave the target for less than 0.3 seconds, the ring waits for you. After that, it starts again from zero.
- You can look at a hole in advance: your eyes count as soon as the target appears.
- A target touched makes a star and a sound, then goes back in its hole. A new one comes out elsewhere, a little later.
- At the top right, the number of targets touched; at the top left, the time left (variants in minutes).
- The speed follows the animation speed of the settings. Nothing moves while the menu (Esc key) is open, and the time stops.

# How to win
There are no lives and no penalty: a target missed simply goes back in its hole. As you chose, the game has no end (leave by the menu (Esc key)), lasts 1 or 3 minutes, or stops after 20 or 50 targets touched: "Bravo", then the statistics.

# The variants
Choose a variant (it is framed), then press "Start".
- Without end, 1 minute, 3 minutes, 20 targets or 50 targets: the end of the game.
- At the bottom, what comes out of the holes: the moles, the Biboules, or My images (your images, cut into a circle; "My images" folder of the settings).
- At the bottom, the level. Easy: 6 holes, 1 target at a time, which stays out 3 seconds longer than the fixation. Normal: 9 holes, 2 targets at a time, 2 seconds longer than the fixation. Hard: 9 holes, 3 targets at a time, 1.5 seconds longer than the fixation.
- At the bottom, how to touch: as soon as looked at, or after a fixation. Without a fixation, the target stays out for the time of the level only.
