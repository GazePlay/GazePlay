# The principle
Aliens in their flying saucers attack! Your rocket destroys them with its lasers, then you have to beat the boss.

# How to play
- The rocket goes, at the bottom of the screen, where you look or where the mouse is. The setting of the inputs decides: with the mouse only, it follows the mouse; with the eye tracker only, it follows the gaze; with both, it follows the last that moved.
- It shoots lasers up by itself.
- An alien hit by a laser is destroyed: a flash, a sound, one more alien.
- The aliens drop red balls: avoid them by moving.
- The rocket has 3 lives (the hearts at the top left). Touched, it loses one and blinks: for 2 seconds, nothing can touch it.
- At the end of the time, or when enough aliens are destroyed, the others go away and the boss comes. It drops three balls at a time. Its life gauge, above it, goes down with each laser.
- The speed follows the animation speed of the settings. Nothing moves while the menu (Esc key) is open, and the time stops.

# How to win
As chosen in the variant: beat the boss, who comes after 1 or 3 minutes of play, or after 20 or 50 aliens destroyed. The boss beaten: "Bravo", then the statistics. Without end: no boss, leave by the menu (Esc key). If the 3 lives are lost: "Lost!", then the statistics.

# The variants
Choose a variant (it is framed), then press "Start".
- Without end, 1 minute then the boss, 3 minutes then the boss, 20 aliens then the boss, 50 aliens then the boss: the end of the game.
- At the bottom, the level. Easy: 4 aliens at a time, each shoots about every 6 seconds; the boss has 15 points of life (15 lasers to beat it).
- Normal: 6 aliens at a time, each shoots about every 4 seconds; the boss has 30 points of life.
- Hard: 8 aliens at a time, each shoots about every 2.5 seconds; the boss has 50 points of life.
