# The principle
Your own images are shown one by one: say whether you like each of them or not.

# Before playing
Add the images in the settings, section Folders, "My images", shared by all the games (or when choosing the game, if there is none yet). They are shown in the order of their names.

# How to play
- The image is in the middle; above it, its number ("3 / 12").
- Look at "I like it" (left) or "I don't like it" (right): a ring goes round the button (fixation length of the settings), then the answer is framed in its color and the next image comes.

# How to win
There is no right answer: every opinion counts. After the last image: "Bravo", then the statistics. Leave earlier with the gear at the bottom left.

# The answers
They are written with the statistics of the game, in a file "...-opinions.csv": the name of each image and the choice ("aime" or "n'aime pas"). An image not answered has no line.

# The variants
One variant only, My images: press "Start".
