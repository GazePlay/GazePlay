# The principle
Drive the blue car with white stripes on the road, catch the stars and avoid the other cars and the barriers of the works!

# How to play
- Look where you want to go: your car goes there, at its speed. It stays on the road.
- On one side of the white line in the middle, the cars overtake you; on the other, they come the other way. You can go from one side to the other. On the line, the cars of both sides can touch you.
- Every 12 seconds (except at the very easy level), a barrier of works comes on the white line in the middle.
- Stars come on the road: drive on them to catch them. Each star caught gives 1 point, with a little sound and "+1".
- Each car passing you without touching you also gives 1 point ("+1"). Every 10 cars avoided: "Great!" with a burst of stars.
- The road goes faster and faster.
- At the top left, your lives; at the top right, your points; in the middle, the time left (variants in minutes).
- The speed follows the animation speed of the settings. At the start, nothing moves during the delay of the settings (1 second by default). Nothing moves while the menu (Esc key) is open, and the time stops.

# How to win
You have 3 lives. If you touch a car or a barrier, you lose a life: your car turns around, the road starts again slowly and, for 4 seconds, your car blinks and fears nothing. After the third life lost, it is "Lost!" with your points. If the time is over before, it is "Bravo". Then come the statistics. With "Without hearts", there are no hearts: the first hit is "Lost!" with your points, then the statistics.

# The variants
Choose a variant (it is circled), then press "Start".
- 1 minute, 3 minutes or without end: without end, the game lasts until the 3 lives are lost (the first hit with "Without hearts").
- At the bottom, the road: horizontal (it goes from left to right) or vertical (it goes up).
- At the bottom too, the level. Very easy: a car every 2.5 seconds, overtaking you slowly (none comes the other way), no barrier; at the start, the road crosses the screen in 10 seconds, and in 6 seconds at its fastest. Easy: 1 or 2 cars a second; at the start, the road crosses the screen in 8 seconds, and in 4 seconds at its fastest. Medium: 2 or 3 cars a second; 6.4 seconds at the start, 2.1 seconds at its fastest. Hard: 3 or 4 cars a second; 4.5 seconds at the start, 1.6 seconds at its fastest. The road reaches its top speed after 50 seconds without a hit.
- At the bottom, the hearts: With hearts (3 hearts) or Without hearts (the first hit loses).
