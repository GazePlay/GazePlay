# The principle
A number is written in the heart of the flower. Make this number with all the petals together.

# How to play
- Each petal takes one kind of values: digits, words, fingers, dice or money. The values of a petal are in the card of its color around the flower.
- Look at a value: a ring goes round it (fixation length of the settings), then it goes into its petal.
- Each value can be used only once: once put, it is pale in its card.
- A petal holds 4 values at most (one word only).
- Look at a value in a petal to take it back.
- At the bottom, "Total" shows the sum of all the petals and the number to make; it turns red when it goes over.

# How to win
When the total makes the number (with the rule of the variant): "Bravo", then a new number. The game does not stop by itself: leave with the gear at the bottom left.

# The variants
Choose a variant (it is framed), then press "Start".
- Up to 10, 20, 30, 40 or 50: the largest number of the heart.
- At the bottom, the mode:
- Guided: the number is shared out among the petals; each petal shows its part ("0 / 4"), turns red when it goes over and green when it makes it.
- Free: only the total counts; some petals can stay empty.
- All the petals: the total, with at least one value in each petal (an empty petal is pale).
