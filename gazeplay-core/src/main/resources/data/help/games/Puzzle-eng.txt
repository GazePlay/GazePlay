# The principle
Put the picture back in order by sliding its pieces into the empty place!

# How to play
- The picture first appears whole, then its pieces are shuffled and one place gets empty. The shuffle can always be solved.
- Fixate a piece next to the empty place (it has a colored border): it slides into the empty place.
- The model stays beside the board to help you.
- A ring fills during a fixation; its length is the one of the settings.
- At the top, the puzzles already solved.
- Nothing can be chosen while the picture is shown and shuffled, nor before the delay of the settings. Nothing moves while the menu (Esc key) is open.

# How to win
When the picture is back in order, its last piece comes back and the puzzle counts a point, then a new puzzle comes. After 1, 3 or 5 puzzles, it is "Bravo", then the statistics. Without end, the game goes on until you leave it by the menu (Esc key).

# The variants
Choose a variant (it is circled), then press "Start".
- 1 time, 3 times, 5 times or without end: the number of puzzles to solve.
- At the bottom, what the pieces show: the numbers, or the pictures of the game (another one at each puzzle).
- At the bottom too, the level: Easy, 3 × 3 a little shuffled; Normal, 3 × 3 fully shuffled; Hard, 4 × 4.
