# The principle
A Biboule wants a magic potion: its bubble shows the recipe, ingredients in pictures. Find them on the shelf and put them into the cauldron!

# How to play
- The bubble of the Biboule shows the recipe: 2, 3 or 4 ingredients (mushroom, feather, star, flower, leaf, apple, crystal, moon, shell, carrot, snowflake, clover).
- On the shelf are the ingredients of the recipe and 2 others, in any order.
- Fixate an ingredient of the recipe: it flies into the cauldron, and it is ticked in green in the bubble. The order does not matter.
- An ingredient out of the recipe shakes and stays on the shelf, greyed: it is a mistake, and you lose a life with the hearts.
- The recipe is complete: the cauldron explodes, the ingredients have merged into a potion. The Biboule drinks it and jumps for joy, then a new client comes.
- A ring fills during a fixation; it lasts the length of the settings.
- At the top left, the lives (a red heart for each life); next to them, the potions served.
- The ingredients fly at the speed of the settings.
- At the start of each new recipe, the first one included, nothing can be chosen during the delay of the settings (Games, "Delay before a selection is possible when new choices appear"); nothing either while an ingredient flies or shakes. Nothing moves while the menu (Escape key) is open.

# How to win
After 3, 5 or 10 recipes, it is "Bravo", then the statistics. You have 3 lives: at the third mistake, it is "Lost!", then the statistics. Without end, the game goes on until you have no life left. With "Without hearts", there are no hearts and the game is never lost: the mistakes are still counted in the statistics; without end, leave by the menu (Escape key).

# The variants
Choose a variant (it is circled), then press "Start".
- 2, 3 or 4 ingredients: the size of the recipes.
- At the bottom, the end: 3, 5 or 10 recipes, or without end.
- At the bottom, the hearts: With hearts (3 hearts) or Without hearts.
