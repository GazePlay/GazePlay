# The principle
Two pictures almost the same: find what changes between them.

# How to play
- Look at a place where the two pictures are not the same (on either picture), long enough (fixation length of the settings).
- If it is a difference, it is circled in green on both pictures, with a sound.
- At the top, "Differences" shows how many you found.
- If you find nothing for 20 seconds, a difference blinks to help you.
- The arrow at the bottom right goes to the next picture.

# How to win
When all the differences of a picture are found: "Bravo", then a new picture (8 scenes, in random order). The game does not stop by itself: leave with the gear at the bottom left.

# The variants
Choose a variant (it is framed), then press "Start".
- Easy: 3 big differences per picture.
- Normal: 5 differences.
- Hard: 7 differences, some of them small (a color that changes, a small object).
