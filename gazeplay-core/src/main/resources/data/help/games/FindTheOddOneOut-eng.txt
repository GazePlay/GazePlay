# The principle
All the pictures are alike but one, the odd one out: find it.

# How to play
- The instruction is written and said, then it goes down to the band at the bottom.
- The pictures appear. Look at the odd one out: a ring goes around the picture while you look at it, and the picture is chosen when it is full (fixation length of the settings).
- To hear the instruction again, look at the loudspeaker of the band.

# How to win
- Right answer: the picture is circled in green, it grows, then "Bravo" and a new question.
- Wrong answer: the picture is crossed out and greyed; look again (the question is said again if the settings ask for it).
- The game does not stop by itself: leave with the gear at the bottom left, or set a time or score limit in the settings (Games section).

# The variants
Choose a variant (it is framed), then press "Start".
- The size of the grid: 4, 6 or 9 pictures. The more pictures, the harder.
