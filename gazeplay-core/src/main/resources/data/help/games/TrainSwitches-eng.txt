# The principle
Trains of colors come out of the tunnel: send each one to the station of its color by changing the switches! Each train carries the same symbol as its station (star, circle, square, heart…).

# How to play
- A switch is a disc on the rails. It shows its two ways: the one the trains will take is dark, with an arrow; the other one is grey.
- Fixate a switch to change its way.
- A train arriving at a switch takes the way shown at that moment.
- A ring fills during a fixation; it lasts the length of the settings.
- At the top, the trains arrived at the right station, and the trains arrived.
- On the hill of the tunnel, a disc shows the next train (its color and its symbol) and the seconds before it leaves: time to get the switches ready. One train at a time, it waits as long as needed: fixate the round button with a train, next to the tunnel, to make it leave.
- The trains go slowly enough to give time to fixate a switch before they reach it: the longer the fixation of the settings, the slower they go. They also follow the speed of the settings. A change made in the menu (Escape key) counts at once.
- Nothing can be chosen before the delay of the settings. Nothing moves while the menu (Escape key) is open.

# How to win
A train arrived at the right station gives one point (a green check). At a wrong station, it is a mistake (a red cross). With 5, 10 or 20 trains and "With hearts", you have 3 hearts at the top left: each train at a wrong station costs one; with no heart left, "Lost!", then the statistics. Without end, there are no hearts and the game goes on. With "Without hearts", there are no hearts and the game is never lost: the mistakes are still counted in the statistics. Every 5 trains, a new circuit comes. After 5, 10 or 20 trains, it is "Bravo", then the statistics. Without end, the game goes on until you leave it with the menu (Escape key).

# The variants
Choose a variant (it is circled), then press "Start".
- 5 trains, 10 trains, 20 trains or without end: the number of trains of the game.
- At the bottom, the level: Easy, 3 stations and 2 switches; Normal, 5 stations and 4 switches; Hard, 8 stations and 7 switches.
- At the bottom too, the rhythm, from the easiest to the hardest: one train at a time and a pause at each switch (both: the mode to learn the game); one train at a time (the next one waits on the tunnel until the last one has arrived and its departure button is fixated); a pause at each switch (everything stops before each switch: change it if needed, then fixate the round button at the right to go on); trains one after the other (always at the same distance).
- At the bottom too, the hearts: Without hearts or With hearts (3 hearts); without end, there are never hearts.
