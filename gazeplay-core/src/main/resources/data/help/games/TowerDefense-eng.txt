# The principle
Little monsters follow the road to your castle. Build towers along the road to stop them!

# How to play
- The waves of monsters start from the arrow and follow the road. Each monster reaching the castle costs you a life (at the top left: the heart, the lives left, and a green bar shorter at each life lost).
- Fixate a grey place by the road: 4 towers are shown around it, with their price. Look at a tower: a card beside says what it does (damage, range, one shot every how many seconds, price). Fixate the one you want to build it, if you have enough coins; a ring fills during the fixation.
- The green tower shoots small balls (25 coins); the red one shoots twice as fast (50 coins); the blue one is a cannon: big slow balls, but very strong, from farther (35 coins); the grey one sends missiles that explode on several monsters, from far (25 coins).
- Each monster destroyed gives you coins. Fixate a tower already built to see what it does, or to sell it (it gives its price back).
- Fixate the snowflake at the top: the blizzard stops the monsters for 5 seconds. It comes back after 30 seconds.
- The waves have 5 then 10 monsters, and get stronger every 2 waves. Every 5 waves come the big violet monsters, every 10 waves the small yellow fast ones.
- The map (the way of the road) is drawn at random at each game, between two.
- The speed follows the animation speed of the settings. Nothing moves while the menu (Esc key) is open.

# How to win
As chosen in the variant: hold 10 or 20 waves, or without end (leave by the menu (Esc key)). The waves held: "Bravo", then the statistics. If all the lives are lost: "Lost!", then the statistics.

# The variants
Choose a variant (it is framed), then press "Start".
- Without end, 10 waves or 20 waves: the end of the game.
- At the bottom, the waves. On demand: a wave starts when you fixate the button "Send the next wave"; you play at your own pace. Automatic: a wave starts by itself 10 seconds after the end of the last one; the button sends it sooner.
- At the bottom too, the level. Easy: 100 coins and 15 lives at the start; the monsters have 15 % more life every 2 waves.
- Normal: 50 coins and 10 lives at the start; 25 % more life every 2 waves.
- Hard: 40 coins and 5 lives at the start; 40 % more life every 2 waves.
