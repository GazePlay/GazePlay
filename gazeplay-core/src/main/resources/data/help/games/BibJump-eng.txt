# The principle
The Biboule bounces from cloud to cloud. Make her climb as high as you can without falling!

# How to play
- Look to the left or to the right of the Biboule: she goes towards your gaze, sideways only. She jumps by herself.
- She only bounces when falling back on a cloud: she goes through it on the way up.
- A red springboard sends her much higher, about two screens up.
- You get 1 point each time she climbs a hundredth of the height of the screen.
- At the top left, your lives; at the top right, your points; in the middle, the time left (variants in minutes).
- The speed follows the animation speed of the settings. Nothing moves while the menu (Esc key) is open, and the time stops.

# How to win
You have 3 lives. If the Biboule falls under the screen, you lose a life: she starts again 1.5 seconds later on a new cloud and you keep your points. After the third fall, it is "Lost!" with your points. If the time is over before that, it is "Bravo". Then come the statistics. With "Without hearts", there are no hearts and the game is never lost: after a fall, the Biboule starts again on a new cloud, and the falls are still counted as mistakes in the statistics; without end, leave by the menu (Esc key).

# The variants
Choose a variant (it gets a frame), then press "Start".
- 1 minute, 3 minutes or without end: without end and with hearts, the game lasts until the 3 lives are lost.
- At the bottom, the level. Easy: wide clouds (a fifth of the screen), close, none moves, slow jumps (1.6 times slower). Medium: medium clouds (a seventh of the screen), further apart, 1 in 4 moves, a little slower jumps (1.25 times). Hard: small clouds (a ninth of the screen), the furthest apart, 1 in 2 moves, fast jumps. The jumps go as high at every level, and the Biboule goes sideways as fast.
- At the bottom, the hearts: With hearts (3 hearts) or Without hearts.
