# The principle
Cards are face down. Only one hides a picture: find it!

# How to play
- Look at a card: a ring goes around the card. When it is full, the card turns over. The length of the ring is that of the settings, also changed in the menu (Esc key).
- A wrong card shows a cross and stays turned over, in grey: you know it is already seen.
- The right card shows the picture: the others fade, the picture grows in the middle with a star and "Well done!". Then other cards come.
- In Magic, the picture is first shown on its card. Then the card turns over and the cards are shuffled before your eyes: follow the card of the picture! When they stop, find it again.
- Nothing is chosen while the cards move.
- At the start, nothing is chosen during the delay of the settings (Games, "Delay before a selection is possible when a game starts"). Nothing moves while the menu (Esc key) is open.

# How to win
There are no lives: a wrong card only counts a mistake in the statistics. As you chose, the game stops after 3, 5 or 10 pictures found ("Bravo", then the statistics), or has no end (leave by the menu (Esc key)).

# The variants
Choose a variant (it is framed), then press "Start".
- The game: Search (the picture is hidden under a card) or Magic (the picture is shown, then the cards are shuffled).
- The number of cards: 3 (in a row), 4, 6 or 9.
- At the bottom, the pictures: animals, or My images (the images of the shared folder, added in the settings, Folders, "My images").
- At the bottom, the end: 3, 5 or 10 pictures, or without end.
