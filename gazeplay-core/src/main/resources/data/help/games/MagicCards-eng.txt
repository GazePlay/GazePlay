# The principle
Pictures are shown on some cards, then the cards turn over and are shuffled before your eyes: find the cards of the pictures again!

# How to play
- Half of the cards have a picture: 1 of 3 cards, 2 of 4, 3 of 6, 4 of 9. They are first shown face up. Then they turn over and the cards are shuffled: follow the cards of the pictures!
- When the cards stop, look at a card: a ring goes around the card. When it is full, the card turns over. The length of the ring is that of the settings, also changed in the menu (Esc key).
- A card of a picture stays face up. A wrong card shows a cross and stays turned over, in grey: you know it is already seen.
- To go on, find all the cards of the pictures: the others fade, a star and "Well done!" (a single picture grows in the middle). Then other cards come.
- Nothing is chosen while the cards move.
- At the start and after each shuffle, nothing is chosen during the delay of the settings (Games, "Delay before a selection is possible when new choices appear"). Nothing moves while the menu (Esc key) is open.

# How to win
A wrong card counts a mistake. With "With hearts", with or without end, you have 3 hearts at the top left: each wrong card costs one; with no heart left, "Lost!", then the statistics. With "Without hearts", there are no hearts and the game is never lost: the mistakes are still counted in the statistics. As you chose, the game stops after 3, 5 or 10 rounds ("Bravo", then the statistics), or has no end (leave by the menu (Esc key)).

# The variants
Choose a variant (it is framed), then press "Start".
- The number of cards: 3 (in a row), 4, 6 or 9.
- At the bottom, the pictures: animals, or My images (the images of the shared folder, added in the settings, Folders, "My images").
- At the bottom, the end: 3, 5 or 10 rounds, or without end.
- At the bottom, the hearts: With hearts (3 hearts) or Without hearts.
