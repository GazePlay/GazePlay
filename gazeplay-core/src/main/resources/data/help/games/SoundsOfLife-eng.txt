# The principle
Listen to the animals: each animal you look at makes its sound, and its name is shown. At sea, the sailing boat rings its bell.

# How to play
- Look at an animal: a ring goes around the animal (fixation length of the settings).
- When the ring is full, the animal makes its sound, swings, and its name is shown above it.
- Look at another animal, or at the same one to hear it again.

# How to win
There is nothing to win: you discover the animals and their sounds, as long as you like. The game does not stop by itself: leave by the menu (Esc key).

# The variants
Choose a variant (it is framed), then press "Start".
- Farm: sheep, cow, horse, pig, rooster and hen.
- Jungle: monkey (a chimpanzee), parrot, leopard, snake and frog.
- Savanna: lion, zebra, elephant and hippopotamus.
- Sea: above the water, a seagull and a sailing boat; the dolphin jumps at the surface; under the water, the whale, the fish, the octopus and the crab.
