# The principle
Make a cake layer by layer: the cake, the cream, an accessory around the layers below, then the sweets and the decoration on the last layer. Copy the model, or make up your own cake!

# How to play
- At the bottom, round buttons show the choices of the step: each button shows the layer as it would be. Look at a button: a ring fills, then the choice goes on the cake. The length of the ring is that of the settings, also changed in the menu (Esc key).
- At the top, the layer and the step of now: Cake, Cream, then Accessory (a ribbon, pearls or hearts around the layer) for the layers below, or Sweets and Decoration for the last layer.
- Copying the model: each choice goes to the next step, then a new layer comes; the cake is compared with the model only once finished (see "How to win").
- The "Undo" button, at the left of the choices, takes the last choice away and goes back to its step; you can undo up to the start of the cake, finished layers included. It only shows once a first choice is made. Copying the model, a wrong cake stays counted as a mistake, and the heart lost does not come back.
- In Hard, the model hides after 5 seconds: look at the button "See the model" to see it again for 3 seconds.
- In Free, after the cream: "One more layer" (the accessory, then a new layer, up to 3) or "Decorate" (the sweets and the decoration, to finish).
- At the start and at each new row of buttons, nothing is chosen during the delay of the settings (Games, "Delay before a selection is possible when new choices appear"). Nothing moves while the menu (Esc key) is open.

# How to win
To copy the model, make the whole cake, then it is compared with the model. If it is not quite the same, "Not quite!": the wrong layers blink on the model, then the cake is taken away and you make the same one again, without the wrong choices. With or without end, with "With hearts", you have 3 hearts at the top left: each wrong cake costs one; with no heart left, "Lost!", then the statistics. With "Without hearts", there are no hearts and the game is never lost: the mistakes are still counted in the statistics. The finished cake shines, with a star and "Well done!". As you chose, the game stops after 1, 3 or 5 cakes ("Bravo", then the statistics), or has no end (leave by the menu (Esc key)).

# The variants
Choose a variant (it is framed), then press "Start".
- The game: Copy the model, or Free (your own cake).
- To copy the model: Easy (1 layer), Normal (3 layers) or Hard (3 layers, hidden model).
- At the bottom, the end: 1, 3 or 5 cakes, or without end.
- At the bottom, to copy the model, the hearts: With hearts (3 hearts) or Without hearts.
