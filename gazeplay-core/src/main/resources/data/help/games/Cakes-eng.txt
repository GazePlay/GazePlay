# The principle
Make a cake layer by layer: the cake, the cream, an accessory around the layers below, then the sweets and the decoration on the last layer. Copy the model, or make up your own cake!

# How to play
- At the bottom, round buttons show the choices of the step: each button shows the layer as it would be. Look at a button: a ring fills, then the choice goes on the cake. The length of the ring is that of the settings, also changed in the menu (Esc key).
- At the top, the layer and the step of now: Cake, Cream, then Accessory (a ribbon, pearls or hearts around the layer) for the layers below, or Sweets and Decoration for the last layer.
- Copying the model: if a choice is not that of the model, the part of the model blinks in red; choose again. A right choice goes to the next step, then a new layer comes.
- In Hard, the model hides after 5 seconds: look at the button "See the model" to see it again for 3 seconds.
- In Free, after the cream: "One more layer" (the accessory, then a new layer, up to 3) or "Decorate" (the sweets and the decoration, to finish).
- At the start, nothing is chosen during the delay of the settings (Games, "Delay before a selection is possible when a game starts"). Nothing moves while the menu (Esc key) is open.

# How to win
There are no lives: a choice that differs from the model only counts a mistake in the statistics. The finished cake shines, with a star and "Well done!". As you chose, the game stops after 1, 3 or 5 cakes ("Bravo", then the statistics), or has no end (leave by the menu (Esc key)).

# The variants
Choose a variant (it is framed), then press "Start".
- The game: Copy the model, or Free (your own cake).
- To copy the model: Easy (1 layer), Normal (3 layers) or Hard (3 layers, hidden model).
- At the bottom, the end: 1, 3 or 5 cakes, or without end.
