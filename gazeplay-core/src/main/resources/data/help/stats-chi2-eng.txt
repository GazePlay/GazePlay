# What this table is for
In the games that use it (the "Where is it?" family), the game checks regularly, by blocks of rounds, whether the right answers could be due to chance. It is a chi-squared test with one degree of freedom. When the answers are not due to chance, the level can go up.

# How to read it
- Block: the number of the block of rounds.
- Level: the level played during this block.
- Observed chi²: the value computed from the right and wrong answers. The larger it is, the less the answers look like chance.
- 50 % to 0.5 %: the risk of being wrong when saying that the answers are not due to chance. A ✓ means that the observed chi² reaches the critical value of this risk; a – that it does not.
- The game uses the risk of 5 % (critical value 3.84) to change the level.
