Default images of the games (gazeplay-core: data/common/default/images).

bear.jpg, dog.jpg, elephant.jpg, elephant2.jpg (a mammoth), fox.jpg, giraffe.jpg, hippo.jpg, lion.jpg, snake.jpg,
turtle.jpg, the same animals without background in gazeplay-games (data/common/default/images/noBackGround) and the
explosion of Magic Potions (data/common/images/explosion.gif):
All emojis designed by OpenMoji (https://openmoji.org/) - the open-source emoji and icon project.
License: CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
Changes: resized, put on a white background (JPEG images), and the explosion made into an animation of 8 frames.
Made in October 2026 by make_default_images.py (this folder), from the 618x618 PNG files of OpenMoji
(https://github.com/hfg-gmuend/openmoji, color/618x618). They replace images of Freepik, which were not free.
These images, and any change to them, stay under CC BY-SA 4.0 (compatible with the GPL version 3 of GazePlay Learning).

Images of Magic Potions (gazeplay-games: data/potions/images): potionRed.png, potionYellow.png, potionBlue.png,
mixingPot.png, background-potions.jpg and the speech bubble of Biboule-Client.png are drawn by make_potions_images.py
(this folder), under the license of GazePlay Learning (GPL-3.0-or-later); Biboule is the one of Biboule Jump. They
replace images of Freepik (October 2026). The script keeps the sizes and the transparent areas the game expects.

Images of Frogs Race (gazeplay-games: data/race/images): the racing frogs (frog-<color>.png, frog-<color>-jump.png),
the frog catching the bugs (frog-catcher.png) and the pond (background.png) are drawn by make_race_images.py (this
folder), under the license of GazePlay Learning (GPL-3.0-or-later). They replace (October 2026) a frog animation and a
landscape of unknown source; Racer.png (a clipart of a commercial site, not free) was not read by the game and is
removed. The flying Biboules (Blue.png ... Red.png, Flash.png) are drawings of the project, kept.

Photos of Where Is The Animal and Where Is The Sound (gazeplay-games: data/whereIsTheAnimal/images,
data/whereIsTheSound/images) and 11 sounds of Where Is The Sound: Wikimedia Commons, under CC0, public domain, CC BY
or CC BY-SA 4.0 (October 2026, see REVUE_DES_JEUX.md). The author, licence and page of each file are in the
CREDITS.txt file of each game (data/whereIsTheAnimal/CREDITS.txt, data/whereIsTheSound/CREDITS.txt), shipped with
the game. The photos were resized to 1280 pixels at most; the sounds shortened to 8 seconds, normalized and made mono.
The triangle of Where Is The Sound (data/whereIsTheSound/images/triangle/triangle_1.jpg) is drawn by
make_triangle_image.py (this folder), under the license of GazePlay Learning (GPL-3.0-or-later).

Thumbnails of the menu of the Where Is It family and of Coloring (gazeplay-core: data/common/Thumbnails: colors.png,
findTheOddOneOut.jpg,
whereIsIt.png, whereIsTheAnimal.png, whereIsTheColor.png, whereIsTheFlag.png, whereIsTheLetter.png,
whereIsTheNumber.png, whereIsTheShape.png, whereIsTheSound.png): captures of the games (October 2026), configuration
button hidden, 960 x 540. The photos they show are those of the games, credited in data/whereIsTheAnimal/CREDITS.txt
(whereIsIt.png shows 4 of them) and data/whereIsTheSound/CREDITS.txt; under their licences (CC BY-SA 4.0 for some).
colors.png shows a coloring page of OpenMoji (CC BY-SA 4.0).

Coloring pages of the game Coloring (gazeplay-games: data/colors/images/<level>/draw-*.png, 8 per level): the black outlines of OpenMoji
(https://openmoji.org/, black/618x618), CC BY-SA 4.0, put on a white background, enlarged and with thicker lines by
make_coloring_images.py (this folder); only emojis whose zones are closed are kept, put in a level folder (easy,
normal, hard) by their number of closed zones (October 2026). They replace 16
drawings of unknown source and a Hello Kitty drawing (not free). The former arrows, palette and buttons of the game
(next.png, prev.png, palette.png, error.png, add-button-inside-black-circle.png) were removed: the game uses the icons
of data/icons (Material Symbols, Apache License 2.0).
